Part 03
The Expansion
1 Year — End of 9th Grade
Finish Off Strong
By the end of 9th grade, I want to finish off strong. I want my grades to be good while my social skills are also improving. Coming into high school I promised myself I will continue to be a good student and to become a better person in terms of my friendships.
⚡ Why — 9th grade is the first step to that goal
3 Years — Graduation
Structure & Understanding
By the time I graduate high school I want more structure in my life. I want to have a clear understanding of who I am and what I want to be. I want to leave high school knowing I achieved a lot and that high school was training for the real world. I want to understand the world far greater than when I entered, build connections with people who will achieve great things, and know that I did what I had to do — and I did a great job at it.
⚡ Why — High school is training for the real world
5 Years — Post-High School
Independence & Growth
In 5 years I will be in my sophomore year of college. At that point in life I hope to gain my independence while understanding the essential aspects of life. I hope to be doing well in my studies, and I hope that my mind would be strong enough to comprehend economics and capable of making money. I want to start making money in a way that is healthy and safe for my future.
⚡ Why — To gain independence and build a future that is mine
